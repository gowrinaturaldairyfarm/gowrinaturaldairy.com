About Gowri Natural Dairy Farm

Our goal is to create a modern, transparent and sustainable dairy enterprise where animal care, clean handling, responsible farming and customer trust work together.

We plan to combine dairy with sheep, goat, fish and native chicken activities over time, creating multiple farm income streams and better resource utilisation.

  • Natural-farming focus
  • Indigenous / A2 breed focus
  • Hygienic milk handling
  • Traceable farm process
  • Rural employment
  • Sustainable resource use
